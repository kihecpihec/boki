# HUD concept

Built-in imagegen tool; ui-mockup. Visual proposal, not an implemented Unity HUD.

## Final prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished game HUD concept image for BOKI: LATE TO FERI, wide 16:9 1920x1080 composition. Full bleed gameplay screenshot with integrated readable HUD, no external presentation frame or annotations. Reference image 1 is environment style reference: angular low-poly spruce forest, coarse PS1 textures, gray blue fog, dirt trail. Reference image 2 is character and scooter identity reference: Boki wears purple jacket with lime stripe, dark trousers, black purple electric scooter. Show third person behind Boki in lower middle riding into a forest clearing, camera looking slightly downward; distant shadow enemies, one small yellow-green battery pickup at side of riding lane. Cold blue-black fog, restrained orange light, tiny purple auto-laser flash. Authentic low-poly indie survival horror, deliberate low resolution texture and vertex geometry, not photorealistic, not cartoon painterly.
HUD design: restrained late-1990s industrial electronics and analog camcorder aesthetic. Off-white sharp condensed monospaced bitmap lettering, charcoal translucent rectangular backing, thin rules, tiny violet and acid-lime accents matching jacket. Legible minimal grain, subtle scanlines in world only, no noise over text. Generous 5% safe margins. Gameplay central 65% unobstructed.
Top left small location label 'POHORJE' with smaller 'A / GOZDNA STRMINA' below.
Top right standalone digital watch display large '07:58', tiny 'FERI' underneath. This is the frozen narrative clock, no countdown.
Bottom left compact grouped two horizontal segmented meters: small medical cross icon and 'HP' with '80 / 100' above muted coral health meter; battery icon and 'BATTERY' with '64 / 100' above acid-lime battery meter. Numbers and labels clearly readable, roughly 260px-wide group.
Bottom center subtle thin violet XP progress strip, small 'UPGRADES 2 / 6' and 'XP 9 / 12'. Progress represents 2 of the 5 XP needed from threshold 7 toward threshold 12, thus 40% full. Small, does not cover character.
Bottom right compact boost module with outlined keycap 'L SHIFT', label 'BOOST', 'READY' and short fully filled violet readiness strip. No speedometer, ammo, minimap, crosshair, firing key, kill quota, quest list, or extra resource.
Near upper center one small restrained red alert line 'GUILT NEARBY' with a simple warning triangle, no large banner, no Guilt health bar. Red reserved for danger. A faint enormous narrow shadow silhouette far between trees suggests danger but does not obscure navigation.
Make the HUD look feasible with simple Unity UI flat sprites and text, strong hierarchy, modest panel size, consistent typography. All specified text exact. Single beautiful actionable HUD mockup, no headings outside gameplay.

## Review

Reviewed the generated image for text, resource values, clear riding space, and consistency with the reference character and forest. HUD values are illustrative. XP 9 / 12 represents 40% progress between cumulative thresholds 7 and 12. The narrative clock stays at 07:58 in Pohorje. Guilt warning wording and layout are proposed presentation.

No Unity assets were added; no Editor import or scene wiring is required for this concept. Actual HUD implementation and interactive readability checks remain future work.
