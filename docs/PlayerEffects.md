# Boki model and effects test

Open `Game/` in Unity **6000.6.7f1**, open `Assets/Scenes/Player_Model_Test.unity` alone, and press Play. This is the existing player model scene (the name is `Player_Model_Test`, not `Plaxer_Model_Test`). Use it for all further Boki model, animation, behavior, and effect demonstrations.

The scene now starts in an isolated enemy-testing arena with the same player. See [enemy sandbox controls and checks](plans/player-test-arena-verification.md). Press Escape to use its panel; **Original movement area** returns to the original ramp and obstacles. The separate stations include contact-collected sandbox XP packs and battery charges. Production XP progression and upgrade selection remain separate work.

For an isolated player setup, open `Assets/Scenes/Boki_Solo_Test.unity`. This separate copy keeps the rigged player, movement, camera, HUD and battery charges on a flat floor with boundary walls. It removes enemy stations and obstacles. Both scenes attach the laser and headlight to the scooter's imported sockets so the effects follow its visual motion. See [solo scene setup and verification](plans/boki-solo-verification.md).

The scene combines the rigged Boki and scooter, riding animation, movement and camera, wheel rotation and visual lean, speed FOV, boost HUD, battery headlight and darkness overlay, recharge pickups, cyan/magenta boost trails, red damage flash, automatic laser with an impact burst, takeoff lean, landing squash, rear-wheel dirt, landing dust, Guilt warning double-pulse/camera pulse, and pickup glows. The shared player prefabs and other developers' map scenes are not modified by the consolidation tool.

## Controls and manual verification

Focus the Game view; click to capture the mouse if needed.

- **WASD / mouse:** ride and steer the camera. **Space:** jump. Test the ramp, airborne steering, and landing hops. The rig should lean back slightly at takeoff, squash briefly on contact, then return to its authored scale. The CharacterController and collision root must keep their original size.
- **Left Shift:** boost. Both short trails should emit only while the controller is actually boosting, then fade. Lean, wheels, FOV, and the HUD should follow movement.
- **H:** take 5 actual damage. Boki and the scooter should flash red briefly and return to their original materials; the health HUD should decrease. Repeated presses restart the flash. At zero health the laser, headlight, and trails stop. Restart Play Mode for a fresh health/battery state.
- **T:** create one stationary laser target five metres ahead, replacing any previous test target. The existing `AutoLaser` should fire at it automatically while it is in range and unobstructed. Each hit shows a 0.08-second beam and an eight-particle cyan impact burst; the target also flashes with existing enemy feedback. The lethal beam remains visible for its full short duration. Press T to repeat. Use an open area so it spawns clear of obstacles.
- **B:** drain 25 battery for testing. Headlight and nearby fill should progressively weaken with the charge fraction; at zero they switch off. Ride over the three original battery charges or a station's three charges to refill the headlight and emit a brief green pickup glow. A full battery must leave a charge available; standing on a depleted charge must not retrigger the glow.
- **G:** preview a red double-pulse on the headlight and wider nearby fill, plus the restrained camera FOV pulse for three active seconds. This does not spawn Guilt or change pressure. The same effects read the shared run's active Warning/Chase phases, excluding pending placement and suspended pressure. Disable `ScooterPresentation.cameraPulseEnabled` in the Inspector to turn off the screen pulse.
- **X:** preview the gold XP glow without awarding XP. Actual increases to `RunSession.State.XP` also trigger it, including contact collection of the enemy sandbox's gold packs. Gold packs now use the shared progression collector and open the real upgrade mockup at the thresholds in [Story §5](Story.md#5-upgrades-and-replay-motivation).
- **Esc:** release the mouse. Test keys require a focused Game view and captured mouse.

Check that trails, laser, headlight, and model render without pink materials or Console errors. Check damage restoration, disable/re-enable cleanup, and that other objects retain their original colors. Windows x86_64 / Ryzen 5600G visual and performance validation remains separate from Linux Editor checks.

## Scene wiring

**Boki → Player Model → Consolidate Effects in Test Scene** refreshes the scene-local setup. Save any dirty scenes and exit Play Mode first. The command opens and saves only `Player_Model_Test`, preserves its existing arena and GUID, and reuses named objects/components on repeated runs. It does not modify build settings, map scenes, or shared prefabs.

After using that legacy consolidation command, run **Boki → Player Model → Create Solo Scene and Fix Scooter Emitters** to restore the scooter socket attachments and slimmer beam in both player scenes.

`BokiPlayerEffects` owns two short rear `TrailRenderer`s and the model's mesh renderers. Trails reuse `Assets/Effects/Boki/BoostTrail.mat`, last 0.25 s, and cast no shadows. Damage uses per-material property blocks, without cloning shared materials. `BokiModelTestEffects` connects the controller's actual boost timer and accepted health decreases to those effects and provides the scene's H/T/B/G/X test controls. It subscribes to the three `BatteryCharge.Collected` events, which fire only after an accepted recharge, and observes XP gains from the shared run. It does not drive the player around automatically.

`AutoLaser` uses a scene-local origin and unshadowed line renderer with the existing laser material; `impactBurst` references the scene's `Laser Impact` particle system. `BokiPlayerEffects.pickupGlow` references `Visuals/Pickup Glow`. Both use `Assets/Effects/Boki/FeedbackParticles.mat` with the existing vertex-color particle shader. The bursts are capped at 16 impact / 32 pickup particles, add no lights, and advance through explicit simulation so shared pause freezes them. Disabling the owning component clears them.

`ScooterPresentation.visualRoot` is the existing non-colliding `Visuals` transform; all lean/squash stays there. `LightBattery` scales its existing spot and fill intensities by charge fraction, retaining the zero-charge gameplay penalty. The warning bridge sets their cosmetic flicker strength and the camera pulse without changing pressure, movement or damage rules. `PlayerHealth`, `LightBattery`, the camera, movement presentation, and HUD retain their existing scene assignments. No additional Inspector setup is required after running the command.

The earlier `BokiEffectsPreview` is a standalone placeholder demonstration. Its effects have been wired onto the real model here; the preview and other scenes were left intact to avoid deleting work or breaking references.

## Player gameplay audio

`PlayerAudio` lives on the shared `Boki_Rigged_Test` player variant used in `Player_Model_Test`, `V1_Pohorje`, `V1_Maribor`, and `V1_FERI`. **Boki → Audio → Apply Player Sounds** updates that prefab and only those four existing scenes; it refuses Play Mode and dirty scenes and restores the prior scene setup. It preserves the maps, gameplay components and their tuning.

The component references `movement`, `health`, `battery`, the scene's `laser`, and three child sources: `motorSource`, `actionSource`, and `rewardSource`. Generated action and warning cues remain in `Assets/Audio/Sfx/Player/`; recorded voices and the replacement electric motor loop come from `Assets/Audio/Sfx/Recorded/`. No Inspector assignments remain after the setup command. Other scenes using the variant inherit movement/health audio; the optional scene-local laser is resolved once on enable when present.

The electric motor loop follows horizontal speed and becomes quieter in the air. Accepted jumps and boosts, airborne landings, and actual laser shots play distinct cues. Wall impacts reuse the landing thud with a speed-scaled gain and a cooldown to avoid repeated scrape sounds. Health decreases play damage or death feedback. Successful battery contacts and XP contacts play separate pickup chimes; rejected/full/depleted contacts remain silent. XP clusters collected in the same frame share one chime. Battery crossings below 20% and down to zero play distinct alerts; recharging rearms them. The existing Guilt light-warning strength drives a double heartbeat, including the model scene's G preview.

Motor/actions pause with shared gameplay pause and focus loss; the motor silences on death or disabled movement. The short death cue can finish behind the death screen. The XP chime can finish when collection opens an upgrade offer; pickup sounds pause for the menu, loading, scripted sequences and focus loss. Disabling the component stops its sources and removes event subscriptions. Sources are player-local 2D sounds, with no distance or Doppler changes from the following camera. `PlayerAudio.volume` is the master tuning field; motor and laser gain are lower so pickups and warnings remain readable.

The generated fallback sounds use original synthesis with no external samples. Regenerate their mono 24 kHz, 16-bit WAV files with `python3 tools/audio/generate_player_sfx.py`; import through Unity 6000.6.7f1 afterward. The setup command imports them as PCM with decompression on load; that generated bank's sample data is under 300 KiB and runtime playback does not synthesize audio.

The recorded voice integration adds three damage variations, nine laser variations, two Guilt voices, Boki's first-warning reaction and the snack recovery voice. Variations avoid an immediate repeat. The scooter motor uses a seamless synthesized electric whirr instead of the recorded vocal loop, retaining the existing speed/pitch presentation. Both motor asset paths contain the replacement so existing scene and prefab references remain valid. The generated jump, boost, landing, death, pickup, battery warnings and heartbeat remain available. **Boki → Audio → Apply Recorded Sounds** updates the existing rigged player variant, `Player_Model_Test`, and the three current V1 scenes without rebuilding them. **Apply Player Sounds** also retains these recorded player bindings when the imported pack is present.

Each of those four scenes has one `RecordedGameAudio` output with six spatial enemy voices and one non-spatial dialogue source. `EnemyAudio` on authored enemies and spawn templates connects accepted damage/death events; the scene-owned sources allow death tails to outlive destroyed enemies. The first active regular enemy can play the recorded greeting. Approaching a level portal within 8 m of its trigger plays one teleport variation per portal per scene visit. The distance is tunable through `RecordedGameAudio.portalVoiceDistance`; cues wait for active gameplay and an idle dialogue source. Enemy voices pause with gameplay and stop on terminal outcomes. The professor's opening question and Boki's reply use the existing shared scripted-sequence pause; the Saturday reveal and Boki reaction continue behind the victory panel. See [recorded SFX wiring and verification](plans/recorded-sfx-verification.md) for assignments, import details and listening steps.
