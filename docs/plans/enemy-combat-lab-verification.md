# Enemy combat test scene

Open `Game/Assets/Scenes/Enemy_Combat_Test.unity` in Unity 6000.6.7f1 and enter Play Mode. This task owns the new scene. It is a separate copy of the eight enemy stations in `Player_Model_Test`, with the movement course, model preview controls, movement HUD, sample XP packs and battery stations removed. Each stage keeps its walls, enemy and any ranged cover. Enemy death rewards and the basic combat HUD remain. The original scene and shared prefabs remain unchanged. Boki remains the controllable combat target; the laser starts disabled so enemies can attack without being killed automatically.

Use the scene panel to select Forest shadow, Izgubljeni meščan, Student shadow, Forest Watcher, City Watcher, Floating exam paper, Guilt, or Professor. Only the selected encounter runs. Esc releases the pointer; click the world to resume riding. Reset enemy respawns the selected encounter; Restart sandbox restores player health, battery and XP.

Open **Boki > Enemies > Open Combat Lab Controls** alongside the Game view for exact damage hits, lethal hits, health readout, active enemy selection, and serialized numeric adapter tuning. These controls are Editor-only. Speed, damage and other directly read fields change on the active instance. Fields captured by an adapter on enable (such as scheduler timings) require disabling and enabling that adapter in the Inspector. Reset respawns from the scene template, discarding active-instance tuning. To retain tuning, exit Play Mode and edit the appropriate object under `Enemy Testing Arena/Inactive Templates` in this scene. Edit maximum HP before spawning. No new Inspector assignments are required.

Guilt intentionally has no health/death test: it is invulnerable under [Story §4](../Story.md#4-enemy-design-and-escalation). Its chase speed and damage come from shared pressure rules, rather than freely editable adapter fields. Test warning, safe placement, chase, contact and repositioning through its station. Wait at least 20 active seconds and turn the camera to allow offscreen placement.

Death testing exposes existing behavior; this change does not replace animation clips or alter combat rules. Melee presentation detaches its animated model to finish the death clip; `EnemyHealth` removes the gameplay root immediately. Watcher and Professor behavior should be assessed separately before choosing animation fixes.
