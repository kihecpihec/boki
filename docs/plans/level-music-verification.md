# Gameplay background music

The three V1 scenes use original, sample-free synth loops generated by
`music/generate_level_music.py` (Python + numpy). Pohorje has subdued 96 BPM
forest techno, Maribor a stronger 120 BPM pulse, and FERI sparse 80 BPM tension.
`Player_Model_Test` demonstrates the Pohorje track.

`MapAmbience` controls a third, non-spatial looping AudioSource, `Gameplay Music`.
It fades in, ducks to 25% during recorded dialogue, pauses during shared gameplay
suspension and focus loss, and stops on death, terminal flow, disable or FERI boss
entry. The existing boss track remains assigned to `V1BossArena`. FERI's gameplay
music uses a lower gain to preserve the quiet approach in Story section 6.
Music is imported as streaming Vorbis to limit decoded audio memory.

To reapply bindings, run **Boki > Audio > Apply Level Music** in Unity 6000.6.7f1
with all scenes saved and Play Mode stopped. This targets existing V1 scenes and
Player_Model_Test without rebuilding them. `MapAmbience.musicSource` references
its child source; `musicVolume` is 0.24 in forest/city and 0.1 in FERI.

Run **Boki > Audio > Run Map Ambience Checks** for source bindings, playback,
pause/focus resume, dialogue ducking, death, re-enable and FERI boss silence.
Manually listen to each level for balance against dialogue, attacks and Guilt,
loop transitions, and the FERI boss handoff. Headless checks do not establish
speaker playback quality or Windows target-hardware performance.
