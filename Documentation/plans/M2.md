## Scope and Starting Point

Use Unity **6000.6.7f1**, the existing Input System and URP, and the delivered M1 movement/camera. Existing assets are documented in [M1 verification](M1-verification.md#assets-and-inspector-assignments). Preserve the approved [movement baseline](../Gameplay.md#m1-movement-baseline).

Implement `AutoLaser`, `EnemyHealth`, `MeleeEnemy`, `PlayerHealth`, and a small scene-local death/retry coordinator with a minimal HP display and death screen. Use one reusable melee prefab and a few manually placed enemies; a spawn director is unnecessary for this milestone.

Routes and `RunSession` belong to M3; persistence belongs to M5. XP/battery drops, upgrades, ranged enemies, Guilt, boss combat, finished HUD, audio, and visual polish are outside M2. Enemy death must leave a clear notification point for future rewards, but must not grant placeholder XP or battery. Full combat/reward flow remains defined in [Architecture §6](../Architecture.md#6-combat-and-enemy-flow).

## Implementation Sequence

### 1. Settle the Combat Baseline

[Gameplay §0](../Gameplay.md#0-gameplay-pillars-and-controls) specifies automatic nearest-enemy targeting; [§6](../Gameplay.md#6-content-and-enemy-progression-by-level) specifies melee pursuit and contact damage. Combat range, fire interval, damage, enemy HP/speed, contact damage interval, and wall occlusion are not yet specified.

Before implementing the affected behavior, agree on these values and whether the laser requires line of sight. If line of sight is required, also decide whether an occluded nearest enemy blocks shooting or whether targeting chooses the nearest visible enemy. Record approved rules and initial tuning in `Gameplay.md`; keep serialized Inspector settings for iteration. Player starting health follows the existing canonical value in [Gameplay §4](../Gameplay.md#4-game-state-and-saving-rules).

Proposed implementation for approval: an instantaneous damage pulse with a brief reusable beam visual, and contact damage limited by a serialized attack interval. Do not add projectile travel, damage invulnerability, attack windups, or other mechanics without a concrete requirement. These proposals are not approved gameplay rules.

### 2. Implement Health and Death Notifications

Create `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/Player/PlayerHealth.cs` and `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/Combat/EnemyHealth.cs`.

- Keep damage calculation separate from UI, movement, and scene reload code. A small shared health helper is reasonable only if it removes actual duplication.
- Initialize valid positive maximum HP, clamp current HP to zero, and ignore non-positive damage and damage after death.
- Expose current/max HP and alive state. Notify listeners of health changes and death; death must fire exactly once, including multiple hits in one frame.
- Enemy death immediately makes the enemy ineligible for targeting and attacks, then removes its object. Do not wait for delayed destruction to mark it dead.
- Let the coordinator handle player death. Do not reload the scene from inside health calculation.
- Keep player health local to M2. In M3, bind it to the canonical `RunSession` state instead of creating two independent HP authorities.

### 3. Implement Automatic Laser

Create `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/Combat/AutoLaser.cs`.

- Select the nearest living regular enemy within the approved range and apply damage at the agreed interval without a fire button.
- Use a small collection of enabled enemies or a bounded physics query. Compare squared distances and avoid scene-wide searches and allocations every frame. Handle enemies with multiple colliders without duplicate candidates or damage.
- Apply the approved occlusion rule. Keep environment collision filtering separate from target eligibility; explicitly exclude the player and beam visual.
- Revalidate a target before firing. Destroyed, disabled, dead, or out-of-range enemies must not receive a shot or retain a stale beam.
- Stop firing when the player dies. Avoid accumulated catch-up volleys after interruptions.
- Use one reusable beam renderer with an inexpensive URP-compatible material, no extra lights, and no collider. Hide it between pulses and when disabled.

### 4. Implement One Melee Enemy

Create `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/Enemies/MeleeEnemy.cs`.

- Pursue an assigned living player using simple movement and primitive collision. For a flat combat arena, start with a `CharacterController`; avoid a navigation package or baked navigation dependency.
- Preserve wall collision and gravity. Do not move through walls by writing directly to the transform. Obstacle routing is outside this flat-arena slice; document the limitation for later level integration.
- Deal damage only within the agreed contact distance and attack interval. Avoid damage once per rendered frame and account for vertical separation while Boki jumps.
- Stop movement and damage immediately when either participant dies or is disabled. Validate missing references without repeated Console errors.
- Use the same script and prefab for all placed melee enemies, with Inspector tuning rather than level-specific AI.

### 5. Implement Death, HP Display, and Retry

Create a small scene-local coordinator under `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/Core/` and a minimal view under `Source/Game/Assets/Scripts/UI/`.

- Subscribe to player health changes and death, and unsubscribe on disable. Display HP through the existing uGUI package; do not add a UI package or the later full HUD.
- On death, disable movement, camera input, automatic firing, and melee activity. Show `ABSENT` and a `Retry` button, and release the cursor.
- Account for M1 behavior: `ThirdPersonCamera` recaptures the cursor on left click, while `ScooterController` gates input on cursor capture. Disable these components before showing the screen so clicking Retry cannot resume dead-player controls.
- Prefer explicit component/activity shutdown over changing global `Time.timeScale`; ensure enemy damage and pending beam callbacks also stop.
- Guard the death transition and Retry action against repeated calls. Retry reloads the combat test scene and restores full HP, initial enemy placement/HP, player spawn, camera, and combat cooldowns.
- Keep retry scene-local, with no persistent singleton or save writes. M3 replaces this reload boundary with `RunSession` new-run initialization; M5 handles permanent unlock preservation.
- Ensure the test scene is available to player builds through an agreed build scene list. Change shared build profiles/settings only with the responsible developer's coordination.

### 6. Assemble and Import in Unity

In the pinned Editor, create the owned combat scene and `Source/Game/Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Melee_Test.prefab`. Reuse the M1 player and arena geometry through Unity, preserving the source assets and their GUIDs. Use a flat combat area for pursuit; keep M1 movement obstacles available for regression checks.

Required Inspector assignments:

- Player: `PlayerHealth`, `AutoLaser`, beam origin/renderer, targeting and environment filters, and approved tuning. Keep existing movement and camera references intact.
- Enemy prefab: primitive visual, simple collision/controller, `EnemyHealth`, and `MeleeEnemy`. Assign the scene player target on each placed instance or through the coordinator; a prefab must not retain scene-object references.
- Coordinator: player health, movement, camera and weapon components, melee instances, HP view, death panel, and Retry button.
- UI: one Canvas and EventSystem using the Input System UI module; wire Retry once. Keep HP readable at 1280×720 and the death panel hidden initially.
- Scene: exactly one gameplay camera and Audio Listener, one main light, clear spawn positions, and a small fixed enemy count within [Gameplay §8](../Gameplay.md#8-performance-target-and-unity-settings) limits.

The M1 player uses `Ignore Raycast`, and the M1 camera obstruction mask includes `Default`. Verify that new enemy layers and filters neither exclude combat targets accidentally nor make enemies obstruct the camera unintentionally. Coordinate shared layer settings before editing them.

Import externally created scripts/assets through Unity **6000.6.7f1** to generate `.meta` files. Preserve existing GUIDs; never fabricate metadata. If Unity cannot run, report pending imports and missing metadata explicitly, and do not describe the assets as ready to integrate.

## Verification and Completion

Add EditMode tests for deterministic health/damage rules: valid initialization, zero/negative damage, nonlethal and lethal damage, overkill, clamping, repeated hits after death, and exactly one death notification. Cover targeting edge cases with EditMode tests if selection is extracted into a deterministic helper; use PlayMode for physics and component lifetime behavior.

The manifest currently has no explicit Unity Test Framework dependency. Check whether EditMode tests can run in the pinned Editor; if adding a package is required, obtain agreement under [Architecture §3 principles](../Architecture.md#3-proposed-modules-and-ownership) before changing the manifest. Do not silently skip required rule tests.

Manual/PlayMode acceptance:

- [x] Scene opens and enters Play Mode without missing references or new Console errors; saved assignments survive reopening.
- [x] Laser fires automatically at the nearest eligible enemy, changes target correctly, obeys range/occlusion decisions, and stops when no valid target exists.
- [x] Enemies with multiple colliders take only the intended damage; dead/disabled/destroyed targets are safely skipped.
- [x] Melee pursues Boki, respects walls, obeys contact distance and attack interval, and behaves correctly when Boki jumps or boosts past it.
- [x] Enemy HP reaches zero once; the enemy stops attacking and disappears. Remaining enemies continue functioning.
- [x] Player HP visibly decreases, cannot fall below zero, and lethal damage shows `ABSENT` exactly once.
- [x] Dead-player movement, look, firing, and enemy damage stop. The cursor stays available for UI clicks and focus changes do not restore gameplay.
- [x] Retry restores initial HP, player/enemy positions, cooldowns, beam state, and controls. Repeat death/retry at least three times without duplicate UI handlers, cameras, or enemies.
- [x] Movement, jump, boost, camera obstruction, and M1 cursor release/recapture still work during live gameplay. Compare attack timing at different frame rates.
- [x] Relevant EditMode tests pass, and the diff contains only intended files, Editor-generated metadata, and agreed shared-asset changes.

Run a Windows x86_64 player check at 1280×720 Low on the Ryzen 5600G when available: automatic combat, death UI, retry scene loading, and a small melee group. Record platform, build settings, enemy count, observed FPS, and failures. Linux checks establish Linux behavior only; M2 does not establish M7 performance acceptance.

Handoff must list approved combat tuning, asset paths, Inspector assignments, scene ownership, checks actually run, remaining Windows/manual checks, and any pending Unity imports. M2 is complete when another developer can open the combat scene, kill a melee enemy with the automatic laser, lose all HP, and retry without repairing assets or encountering new Console errors.
