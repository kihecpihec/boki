## Implementation Sequence

### 1. Establish Run Outcome and Battery Rules

- Extend the existing run authority with a small explicit outcome lifecycle: exploring, boss active, won, or lost. Keep transitions deterministic and testable rather than deriving victory from a UI object.
- Accept arena entry only for a living player with an active FERI choice, once per run. Preserve M5's immediate unlock save and unchanged current-run snapshot. Start the boss only after that validated entry; duplicate contact must not repeat saving, initialization, or spawning.
- Record `POWER OFF` use as temporary run state. Accept it only on selected FERI C, before arena entry, for the living player. Clear it on New Run; never persist it as an unlock.
- Keep one authoritative battery value across scenes. Use fractional charge or an accumulator so 1.5/s drain is independent of frame rate; clamp charge to capacity and reject invalid gains/time deltas. Start New Run at 100.
- Use [Story §4](../Story.md#4-enemy-design-and-escalation) initial battery tuning: regular kills +10, pickups +25, and +30% enemy movement speed at zero charge without increased damage. Resume ordinary speed immediately after recharge.
- Drain only during active gameplay, including the boss. Stop drain, attacks, rewards, and outcome changes after win/loss; preserve the existing cursor/focus behavior. Do not add an unrelated pause-menu system.
- Keep standalone M1–M3 scenes independent of M6 initialization and rewards. Retry/New Run clears temporary state and reads saved unlocks exactly as M5 does.

### 2. Add Shared Ranged Enemies and Projectiles

- Implement one `RangedEnemy` and reusable `EnemyProjectile` for Watchers, Red Watchers, flying exam papers, and the professor's barrage. Use scene/material variants rather than separate AI systems.
- Fire slow, visible projectiles toward the living player on a cooldown with a clear range and line-of-sight check. Avoid shots through walls; skip inactive, dead, or terminal-run targets.
- Resolve projectile travel with a swept collision query so fast frames cannot tunnel through thin walls or the player. Ignore the shooter and unrelated triggers; the first blocking environment/player hit consumes the projectile and applies damage at most once.
- Give each projectile a finite lifetime/range. Clear outstanding projectiles on death, victory, and scene unload. Keep population bounded by firing intervals and lifetimes; add pooling only if profiling shows a need.
- Use existing `EnemyHealth`/AutoLaser targeting for killable ranged enemies. Keep immortal Guilt out of that registry. Retain nearest-visible-target and laser occlusion behavior.
- Expose unspecified projectile damage, speed, range, and cooldown as Inspector tuning. Record the values actually used in the M6 handoff; do not claim untested balance.

### 3. Integrate Light, Rewards, and Pickups

- Add `LightBattery` as a scene adapter to run battery state, not a second persistent store. Bind a simple unshadowed scooter Spot Light and readable battery display.
- At zero charge, visibly extinguish the light and apply the canonical movement multiplier to regular enemies and Guilt. Keep laser damage/fire rate, player movement, and enemy damage unchanged so kills remain a recovery path. The stationary professor has no movement bonus.
- Award battery and increment kill count exactly once for each regular enemy death in production gameplay. Boss death triggers victory separately, without a regular-enemy reward. Preserve standalone M2 behavior.
- Add contact battery pickups that accept the living player once, clamp at capacity, and remain inactive in unchosen branches. Forest C receives its guaranteed battery pickup; Lent gets visibly placed battery recovery opportunities.
- Keep recharge possible at zero battery through the working laser and reachable pickups. Verify that scene transitions retain partial charge and cannot duplicate death rewards or collection.

### 4. Implement Guilt and Panic Movement

- Implement `Distant → Warning → Chase → Reposition`, following [Gameplay §5](../Gameplay.md#5-loop-within-each-route). Use two or three explicit scene/route anchors and simple collision-aware movement; do not add navigation packages.
- Show a readable warning before chase, with restrained light flicker and a minimal visual cue if no approved audio exists. Restore the normal battery-driven light after the warning.
- Reposition only to valid offscreen anchors behind the player. If none is safe, delay the appearance; never visibly teleport into the player or across a locked branch. Detect stuck or excessively distant pursuit and return to reposition.
- Guilt is invulnerable and absent from laser targeting. Contact deals ordinary configurable damage on a cooldown with wall occlusion, never instant death.
- Grant the canonical +25% panic movement for 2 seconds with a 5-second cooldown when nearby. Apply it as a temporary multiplier to the existing controller, including its existing boost, without changing base tuning or repeatedly stacking bonuses.
- Keep the short Forest chase, longer City pursuit, aggressive Podzemni prehod event, and final FERI corridor appearance within selected branches. Build the canonical City C turn-back: a dead end and Guilt appearance ahead, a rear door closing, and a side opening available only after the event starts. Complete escape before allowing the City exit.
- On arena entry disable all pursuit/contact logic and clear panic effects; retain only the decorative boss-room silhouette. Death, victory, disabling, and Retry must also clear effects and timers.

### 5. Wire Production Encounters and the POWER OFF Panel

After scene ownership is confirmed:

- Pohorje: retain the safe opening and melee emphasis; add the late Watcher on Gondola, Forest C's two stronger regular enemies and guaranteed pickup, and short Guilt events.
- Maribor: retain Tabor's melee pressure; add more ranged pressure and battery pickups to Lent, plus the selected-only Guilt set piece in Podzemni prehod.
- FERI: retain A/B identity and add floating-paper ranged variants where readable. Give C two simple telegraphed floor hazards with primitive trigger areas and bounded damage cooldowns.
- Place a functional `POWER OFF` panel before the classroom on C. Show `E — POWER OFF` only within interaction range for an eligible player with unobstructed access. Successful use is idempotent and visibly confirmed; out-of-range, wrong-route, dead, and post-entry use must fail.
- Suppress unchosen branch interior rendering after selection while retaining gate/rear-seal collision and visuals. At arena entry, suppress approach rendering, deactivate its encounters, and retain a solid entrance seal plus arena perimeter.
- Keep branch enemies, pickups, hazards, and chase events under selected-only encounter activation. Unchosen content cannot attack, grant rewards, become targets, or leak through the shared merge.
- Stay within the [regular-enemy caps](../Gameplay.md#6-content-and-enemy-progression-by-level), including boss reinforcements. Keep total active FERI regular enemies at or below eight when two reinforcements appear.

### 6. Build the Professor Fight in the Shared Classroom

- Add one professor, initially inactive, using a killable health target compatible with AutoLaser. Ensure the arena and professor's aim point are reachable by the existing 10 m laser; keep decorative lectern geometry from blocking the entire fight.
- On validated classroom entry, save the existing FERI unlock, close the arena entrance behind the player, stop approach threats/projectiles, disable Guilt AI, and initialize the fight once. Keep room for steering, jump, and boost; avoid sealing a collider through the player.
- Use 4000 maximum HP after the duration pass, matching the approved laser cadence. FERI A starts normally. FERI C starts at 85% HP only if the panel was used; otherwise it starts normally. Preserve maximum HP for correct display and phase thresholds.
- Alternate `Exam Barrage` (three slow papers using the shared projectile system) with `Deadline Slam` (a visible ground circle locked to a position, warning for 0.8 seconds, then one bounded area hit). Slam must be escapable with the approved boost and cannot damage through arena walls.
- At the first crossing of 50% maximum HP, slightly shorten attack intervals once. On FERI B, activate exactly two preplaced melee reinforcements once, even if a hit skips across the threshold. Other routes never receive that reinforcement event.
- Gate all attacks on the active fight and living player. Cancel pending slam damage and clear projectiles on boss death/player death. Define simultaneous lethal outcomes consistently: accept only the first valid terminal transition.
- Display professor HP, the current warning, and a clear objective. Keep Guilt as a visual silhouette, not a second combatant.

### 7. Complete Victory, Death, and New Run

- Boss death transitions to victory once. Stop the weapon, enemy/hazard/projectile damage, movement, battery drain, and chase logic; release the cursor for UI.
- Show a minimal ending with the preserved lines »Boki ... danes je sobota.« and »... A?«, followed by `YOU SURVIVED. YOU WERE NEVER LATE.` No cutscene is required.
- Show chosen routes, elapsed time frozen at the outcome, kills, and permanent discoveries. Keep current-run available unlocks separate from newly earned progress. Do not display invented collected upgrades.
- Reuse `ABSENT` and Retry for death in every level and during the boss. Generalize shutdown beyond the current melee-only coordinator without breaking M2's standalone retry.
- `NEW RUN` after victory and Retry after death reset the existing session and load Pohorje with fresh HP, battery, cooldowns, enemies, outcome, and panel state. Preserve permanent unlocks and adopt them only in the new snapshot.

### 8. Inspector Wiring, Helpers, and Import

Required saved assignments:

- Battery adapter: session/player, Spot Light, HUD Text, and pickup/reward bindings.
- Ranged enemies: player, health, firing origin, projectile visual/configuration, obstruction mask, and route encounter root.
- Guilt: player/controller, warning view/light, valid route anchors, simple collision geometry, and arena shutdown binding.
- Panel/traps: selected-route eligibility, player, interaction action/range, prompt/feedback, and visible hazard area/timing.
- Arena: existing arrival/route manager, professor health/aim point, projectile origins, slam marker, entrance seal, decorative silhouette, two inactive reinforcement instances, HP/warning/result UI, and New Run button.
- Combat lifecycle: all new threats consult the shared outcome and are shut down on death/victory; no duplicate session, camera, AudioListener, EventSystem, or reward subscription.

Add an explicit three-scene M6 build helper without changing shared build settings. Import new scripts/assets with Unity **6000.6.7f1** to generate `.meta` files; never invent GUIDs. If the Editor cannot run, document pending imports and missing metadata and do not call the slice ready to integrate.
